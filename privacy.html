Privacy Policy

Temple of Diamonds · Last updated 2 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Temple of Diamonds (shown on the home screen as "Diamond Temple") handles, on Android and iOS. The game is made by an independent developer, referred to below as "we".

The short version

  • The game has no accounts, and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • Your progress (stages, diamonds, lives, settings) is saved only on your device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device and usage information to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress and settings (sound, music, the stage you reached, your diamonds and lives, and your ad consent choice) in the device's own app storage. We do not receive this information; it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: ads you choose to watch for a reward (for example an extra life) and occasional ads between stages. To serve, measure and protect those ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID or, if you allow it, Apple's IDFA;
  • Your IP address and the approximate location it implies;
  • App interactions with ads (ads shown and tapped) and diagnostics such as crash and performance data;
  • Basic device information such as model, operating system and language.

Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Its use is governed by Google's own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Options → Privacy settings.
  • iOS tracking: the game asks Apple's "Allow tracking" question. If you say no, ads are not personalised using the IDFA. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in Android Settings → Google (or Privacy) → Ads.
  • Choosing not to have personalised ads does not reduce the game's features; you may still see non-personalised ads.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the advertising data handled by Google as described above, the game sends no personal information to us or to anyone else.

Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the game. Data collected by Google for advertising is kept according to Google's policies; you can manage it through your device's ad settings and your Google account.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
